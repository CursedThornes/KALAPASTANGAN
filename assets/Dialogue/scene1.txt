# KALAPASTANGAN - Scene 1: The Hallway
# Commands: !expr, !shake, !flash, !set flag, !if flag -> label
#            !trust who +/-n, !mind who +/-n
#            !iftrust who >= n -> label, !ifmind who >= n -> label
#            !timed seconds -> timeoutLabel, !end
# Text effects: {shake}, {wave}, {red}, {gray}, {slow}, {wait=0.5}

[start]
!chapter FOOL | The Hallway
!expr normal
!trust maria =4
!mind maria =3
: The hallway was unusually quiet.
: The only sounds were the faint hum of the fluorescent lights and the occasional creaking of the old building.
MARIA: Mark?
MARIA: May narinig ka ba?
MARK: Hm? Ano 'yon?
MARIA: Parang may naglalakad sa may hagdan.
MARK: Baka estudyante lang.
MARIA: Mark, wala na halos tao rito.
MARK: Eh, baka may naiwan lang na teacher.
MARIA: At this hour? Seriously?
: Maria glanced toward the staircase. For a moment, she seemed hesitant.
MARIA: ...Tingnan kaya natin?
MARK: Bakit ba kailangan pa nating alamin?
MARIA: Curious lang ako!
MARK: Curious ka lang, pero ako 'yung pinapapunta mo?
MARIA: HAHAHA! Tara na nga!
MARK: Tsk. Sige na.

* Sige. Pero ikaw ang mauuna. -> teasing
* Paano kung may tao nga talaga roon? -> cautious
* Uwi na tayo. Huwag na. -> reluctant

[teasing]
!set teasing
!trust maria +1
!mind maria +1
MARK: Sige, pero ikaw ang mauuna.
MARIA: Hala! Bakit ako?!
MARK: Eh, ikaw naman ang may gustong pumunta.
MARIA: Grabe ka talaga. Sige na nga!
-> footsteps

[cautious]
!set cautious
!trust maria +2
MARK: Paano kung may tao nga talaga roon?
MARIA: Eh di malalaman natin!
MARK: That's not exactly reassuring.
MARIA: HAHAHA! Huwag kang matakot. Nandito naman ako.
-> footsteps

[reluctant]
!set reluctant
!trust maria -1
!mind maria +2
MARK: Pwede bang umuwi na lang tayo?
MARIA: Ang aga pa!
MARK: Maaga? Halos wala nang tao sa school.
MARIA: Five minutes lang! Promise!
MARK: 'Yan din sinabi mo kanina.
MARIA: This time, promise talaga!
-> footsteps

[footsteps]
: Before you could say anything else, another sound echoed from the staircase.
: Footsteps.
: Slow, uneven footsteps.
MARIA: ...
MARK: Narinig mo 'yon?
MARIA: Oo.
: Maria's playful expression slowly faded.
!expr nakikinig
MARIA: Mark... may tao ba talaga roon?
MARK: Hindi ko alam.
: You both stood still, listening.
: Then, another step.
: And another.
MARIA: ...Hindi na yata magandang ideya 'to.
MARK: Ikaw ang may gusto nito, 'di ba?
MARIA: Alam ko na nga!
!shake
: A faint shadow appeared at the far end of the staircase.
!flash
: For a brief moment, neither of you moved.
MARIA: {shake}Mark...{/shake}
: The shadow did not leave.
MARIA: {red}Mark.{/red} {slow}Ano gagawin natin?{/slow}

!timed 6.5 -> froze
* Hawakan si Maria. -> hold
* Tawagin kung sino man 'yon. -> call
* Huwag kang gagalaw. -> stay

[hold]
!set held_maria
!trust maria +2
!mind maria -1
: You took her wrist before either of you could think.
MARK: Don't move.
MARIA: ...Salamat.
: Her pulse was fast. She did not pull away.
-> afterCrisis

[call]
!set called_out
!trust maria -1
!mind maria +3
!shake
MARK: Sino'ng andun?!
MARIA: Mark, {shake}tigil!{/shake}
: The footsteps stopped. Then they started again, closer.
-> afterCrisis

[stay]
!set stayed_still
!mind maria +1
: You did not move. You barely breathed.
MARIA: ...Mark?
MARK: Wait.
: The shadow lingered at the edge of the light.
-> afterCrisis

[froze]
!set froze
!trust maria -2
!mind maria +2
: You stayed silent until the moment passed. Maria had to act on her own.
MARIA: Mark? {shake}Mark!{/shake}
: She grabbed your sleeve herself, unsure whether you would respond.
-> afterCrisis

[afterCrisis]
: The shadow disappeared.
MARK: ...Nakita mo rin?
MARIA: Oo.
!iftrust maria >= 6 -> close
-> distant

[close]
: Maria stepped into your space and stayed there.
MARIA: Huwag mo akong iwan.
MARK: Hindi.
-> mentalFork

[distant]
: Maria slowly stepped closer, then stopped short of you.
MARIA: Please tell me may explanation ka para roon.
MARK: Kung meron man, hindi ko rin alam.
-> mentalFork

[mentalFork]
!ifmind maria >= 7 -> breaking
!ifmind maria >= 5 -> frightened
-> uneasy

[uneasy]
!expr nakikinig
: The hallway fell silent once more.
MARIA: ...Baka ilaw lang. Baka.
MARK: Hindi yata.
: Neither of you wanted to be the first to walk away.
-> ending

[frightened]
!expr nakikinig
!shake
: Maria's hands would not stay still.
MARIA: {shake}Hindi ko gusto 'to, Mark.{/shake}
MARIA: Parang... may tumingin sa atin.
MARK: Kung meron, nandito pa rin.
: The quiet after that felt heavier than the footsteps.
-> ending

[breaking]
!expr nakikinig
!shake
!flash
: She was shaking hard enough that you could hear it in her voice.
MARIA: {red}{shake}Iuwi mo na ako.{/shake}{/red}
MARIA: Please. Ngayon na.
MARK: Sige. Sige, alis na tayo.
: But the hallway did not feel finished with you.
-> ending

[ending]
!if froze -> lastFroze
!if called_out -> lastCall
!if held_maria -> lastHold
-> lastStay

[lastHold]
: She still had not let go of you.
!battle liyab
-> afterBattle

[lastCall]
: Somewhere below, something had heard its name.
!battle liyab
-> afterBattle

[lastStay]
: You had waited. It had waited too.
!battle liyab
-> afterBattle

[lastFroze]
: You froze once. The dark remembered.
!battle liyab
-> afterBattle

[afterBattle]
!expr nakikinig
: The hallway was quiet again. The hum of the lights came back first, then the smell of dust.
: Maria was still holding her own sleeve, breathing like she had run up the stairs.
MARIA: ...Mark.
MARK: Andito lang ako.
MARIA: Nakita mo ba 'yon? Yung... yung hindi na tao.
MARK: Oo.
: She did not ask anything else. She just listened to a building that had gone too quiet.
!shake
MARIA: {shake}May narinig ka pa?{/shake}
: Somewhere below the stairs, something was moving again. Slowly. Deliberately.
: Not footsteps this time. Something like a voice, practicing your name.
!expr normal
-> tellOrHide

[tellOrHide]
* Sabihin sa kanya ang totoo. -> honest
* Basta umalis na tayo. -> leaveQuiet
* Tanungin siya kung ano ang nakita niya. -> askHer

[honest]
!set told_truth
!trust maria +2
!mind maria -1
MARK: Hindi lang ilaw 'yon, Maria.
MARK: May bumangon doon. At hindi pa tapos.
MARIA: Alam ko.
: She looked at you for a long time and did not look away.
MARIA: Salamat sa pagsabi ng totoo.
-> secondBeat

[leaveQuiet]
!set hid_truth
!trust maria -2
!mind maria +2
MARK: Wala 'yon. Baka pagod lang tayo.
MARIA: ...
MARIA: Sige. Kung 'yan ang gusto mong paniwalaan.
: She did not sound convinced. She did not let go of your sleeve either.
-> secondBeat

[askHer]
!set asked_her
!trust maria +1
!mind maria +2
MARK: Ano'ng nakita mo?
MARIA: Hindi ko alam kung paano sasabihin.
MARIA: Parang... kilala niya ako.
MARK: Kilala?
MARIA: Huwag na. Please. {slow}Huwag na nating pag-usapan.{/slow}
-> secondBeat

[secondBeat]
!expr nakikinig
: The voice from below got clearer. It was not calling anyone in particular.
: It was counting.
MARK: Maria, kung may mangyari...
MARIA: Huwag mo akong seseryosohan ng ganyan.
!shake
MARIA: {shake}Hindi ako aalis nang wala ka.{/shake}
-> stayOrGo

[stayOrGo]
* Hawakan mo ang kamay niya at bumaba. -> together
* Sabihin mong maghintay siya. Ikaw ang lalabas. -> alone
* Sabihin mong siya ang mauna. -> sendHer

[together]
!set together
!trust maria +2
: You took her hand. She held on like it was the only thing keeping her standing.
MARK: Sabay.
MARIA: Sabay.
: At the bottom of the stairs, the air had weight.
-> awaken

[alone]
!set alone
!trust maria -3
!mind maria +1
MARK: Hintayin mo ako dito.
MARIA: Mark, hindi—
MARK: Maria. Please.
: She let go first. You wished she had not.
: You went down alone. The dark did not seem to mind.
-> awaken

[sendHer]
!set sent_her
!trust maria -2
!mind maria +1
MARK: Ikaw ang mauna. Ako ang susunod.
MARIA: Ayokong umalis nang ganito.
MARK: Maria, please.
: For a moment she did not move. Then she turned, and the stairs took her slowly.
: You told yourself she would be safer. You were not sure you believed it.
-> awaken

[awaken]
!expr nakikinig
!flash
!shake
: The shadow at the bottom of the stairs had changed shape.
: It had a face now. It had your face, almost.
MARIA: (from behind you) Mark— get back—
!expr normal
!battle dilim
: The silence after the second battle broke in a voice that sounded like the building itself.
MARIA: Mark... hindi pa tapos.
MARK: Alam ko.
: The hallway answered with a cry from somewhere below the stairs.
!chapter SIGAW | The Last Echo
!expr normal
MARIA: This time, sabay tayo.
MARK: Sabay.
!battle sigaw
!end
