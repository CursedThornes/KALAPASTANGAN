# KALAPASTANGAN - Scene 1: The Hallway
# Commands: !expr, !shake, !flash, !set flag, !if flag -> label
#            !trust who +/-n, !mind who +/-n
#            !iftrust who >= n -> label, !ifmind who >= n -> label
#            !timed seconds -> timeoutLabel, !end
# Text effects: {shake}, {wave}, {red}, {gray}, {slow}, {wait=0.5}

[start]
!chapter FOOL | The Hallway
!expr normal
!trust maria =4
!mind maria =3
: The hallway was unusually quiet.
: The only sounds were the faint hum of the fluorescent lights and the occasional creaking of the old building.
MARIA: Mark?
MARIA: Did you hear that?
MARK: Huh? What is it?
MARIA: I think someone's walking by the stairs.
MARK: It's probably just a student.
MARIA: Mark, there's hardly anyone left at school.
MARK: Maybe a teacher stayed late.
MARIA: At this hour? Seriously?
: Maria glanced toward the staircase. For a moment, she seemed hesitant.
MARIA: ...Should we go take a look?
MARK: Why do we have to find out?
MARIA: I'm just curious!
MARK: You're curious, but I'm the one who has to go?
MARIA: Hahaha! Come on, let's go!
MARK: Fine. Let's go.

* Fine, but you go first. -> teasing
* What if someone's really there? -> cautious
* Can we just go home instead? -> reluctant

[teasing]
!set teasing
!trust maria +1
!mind maria +1
MARK: Fine, but you go first.
MARIA: Hey! Why me?!
MARK: You're the one who wanted to come.
MARIA: You're the worst. Fine, let's go.
-> footsteps

[cautious]
!set cautious
!trust maria +2
MARK: What if someone's really there?
MARIA: Then we'll find out!
MARK: That's not exactly reassuring.
MARIA: Hahaha! Don't be scared. I'm right here.
-> footsteps

[reluctant]
!set reluctant
!trust maria -1
!mind maria +2
MARK: Can we just go home?
MARIA: It's still early!
MARK: Early? There's hardly anyone left at school.
MARIA: Five minutes lang! Promise!
MARK: You said that earlier.
MARIA: I really mean it this time!
-> footsteps

[footsteps]
: Before you could say anything else, another sound echoed from the staircase.
: Footsteps.
: Slow, uneven footsteps.
MARIA: ...
MARK: Did you hear that?
MARIA: Yeah.
: Maria's playful expression slowly faded.
!expr nakikinig
MARIA: Mark... is someone really there?
MARK: I don't know.
: You both stood still, listening.
: Then, another step.
: And another.
MARIA: ...This might not have been a good idea.
MARK: You wanted to come here, remember?
MARIA: I know!
!shake
: A faint shadow appeared at the far end of the staircase.
!flash
: For a brief moment, neither of you moved.
MARIA: {shake}Mark...{/shake}
: The shadow did not leave.
MARIA: {red}Mark.{/red} {slow}What do we do?{/slow}

!timed 6.5 -> froze
* Take Maria's hand. -> hold
* Call out to whoever's there. -> call
* Stay still. -> stay

[hold]
!set held_maria
!trust maria +2
!mind maria -1
: You took her wrist before either of you could think.
MARK: Don't move.
MARIA: ...Thanks.
: Her pulse was fast. She did not pull away.
-> afterCrisis

[call]
!set called_out
!trust maria -1
!mind maria +3
!shake
MARK: Who's there?!
MARIA: Mark, {shake}stop!{/shake}
: The footsteps stopped. Then they started again, closer.
-> afterCrisis

[stay]
!set stayed_still
!mind maria +1
: You did not move. You barely breathed.
MARIA: ...Mark?
MARK: Wait.
: The shadow lingered at the edge of the light.
-> afterCrisis

[froze]
!set froze
!trust maria -2
!mind maria +2
: You stayed silent until the moment passed. Maria had to act on her own.
MARIA: Mark? {shake}Mark!{/shake}
: She grabbed your sleeve herself, unsure whether you would respond.
-> afterCrisis

[afterCrisis]
: The shadow disappeared.
MARK: ...Did you see that too?
MARIA: Yeah.
!iftrust maria >= 6 -> close
-> distant

[close]
: Maria stepped into your space and stayed there.
MARIA: Don't leave me.
MARK: I won't.
-> mentalFork

[distant]
: Maria slowly stepped closer, then stopped short of you.
MARIA: Please tell me there's an explanation for that.
MARK: If there is, I don't know it.
-> mentalFork

[mentalFork]
!ifmind maria >= 7 -> breaking
!ifmind maria >= 5 -> frightened
-> uneasy

[uneasy]
!expr nakikinig
: The hallway fell silent once more.
MARIA: ...Maybe it was just the lights. Maybe.
MARK: I don't think so.
: Neither of you wanted to be the first to walk away.
-> ending

[frightened]
!expr nakikinig
!shake
: Maria's hands would not stay still.
MARIA: {shake}I don't like this, Mark.{/shake}
MARIA: It feels like... something's watching us.
MARK: If it is, it's still here.
: The quiet after that felt heavier than the footsteps.
-> ending

[breaking]
!expr nakikinig
!shake
!flash
: She was shaking hard enough that you could hear it in her voice.
MARIA: {red}{shake}Take me home.{/shake}{/red}
MARIA: Please. Right now.
MARK: Okay. Okay, let's go.
: But the hallway did not feel finished with you.
-> ending

[ending]
!if froze -> lastFroze
!if called_out -> lastCall
!if held_maria -> lastHold
-> lastStay

[lastHold]
: She still had not let go of you.
!battle liyab
-> afterBattle

[lastCall]
: Somewhere below, something had heard its name.
!battle liyab
-> afterBattle

[lastStay]
: You had waited. It had waited too.
!battle liyab
-> afterBattle

[lastFroze]
: You froze once. The dark remembered.
!battle liyab
-> afterBattle

[afterBattle]
!expr nakikinig
: The hallway was quiet again. The hum of the lights came back first, then the smell of dust.
: Maria was still holding her own sleeve, breathing like she had run up the stairs.
MARIA: ...Mark.
MARK: I'm right here.
MARIA: Did you see it? The... thing that wasn't human anymore?
MARK: Yeah.
: She did not ask anything else. She just listened to a building that had gone too quiet.
!shake
MARIA: {shake}Did you hear that?{/shake}
: Somewhere below the stairs, something was moving again. Slowly. Deliberately.
: Not footsteps this time. Something like a voice, practicing your name.
!expr normal
-> tellOrHide

[tellOrHide]
* Tell her what really happened. -> honest
* Pretend it was nothing. -> leaveQuiet
* Ask her what she saw. -> askHer

[honest]
!set told_truth
!trust maria +2
!mind maria -1
MARK: It wasn't just the lights, Maria.
MARK: Something woke up down there. And it's not over.
MARIA: I know.
: She looked at you for a long time and did not look away.
MARIA: Thanks for telling me the truth.
-> secondBeat

[leaveQuiet]
!set hid_truth
!trust maria -2
!mind maria +2
MARK: It was nothing. Maybe we're just tired.
MARIA: ...
MARIA: Fine. If that's what you want to believe.
: She did not sound convinced. She did not let go of your sleeve either.
-> secondBeat

[askHer]
!set asked_her
!trust maria +1
!mind maria +2
MARK: What did you see?
MARIA: I don't know how to explain it.
MARIA: It felt like... it knew me.
MARK: Knew you?
MARIA: Never mind. Please. {slow}Let's not talk about it.{/slow}
-> secondBeat

[secondBeat]
!expr nakikinig
: The voice from below got clearer. It was not calling anyone in particular.
: It was counting.
MARK: Maria, if anything happens...
MARIA: Don't say it like that.
!shake
MARIA: {shake}I'm not leaving without you.{/shake}
-> stayOrGo

[stayOrGo]
* Go downstairs with her. -> together
* Go down alone and tell her to wait. -> alone
* Tell her to go first. -> sendHer

[together]
!set together
!trust maria +2
: You took her hand. She held on like it was the only thing keeping her standing.
MARK: Together.
MARIA: Together.
: At the bottom of the stairs, the air had weight.
-> awaken

[alone]
!set alone
!trust maria -3
!mind maria +1
MARK: Wait here for me.
MARIA: Mark, no—
MARK: Maria. Please.
: She let go first. You wished she had not.
: You went down alone. The dark did not seem to mind.
-> awaken

[sendHer]
!set sent_her
!trust maria -2
!mind maria +1
MARK: You go first. I'll follow.
MARIA: I don't want to leave like this.
MARK: Maria, please.
: For a moment she did not move. Then she turned, and the stairs took her slowly.
: You told yourself she would be safer. You were not sure you believed it.
-> awaken

[awaken]
!expr nakikinig
!flash
!shake
: The shadow at the bottom of the stairs had changed shape.
: It had a face now. It had your face, almost.
MARIA: (from behind you) Mark—get back—
!expr normal
!battle dilim
: The silence after the second battle broke in a voice that sounded like the building itself.
MARIA: Mark... it's not over.
MARK: I know.
: The hallway answered with a cry from somewhere below the stairs.
!chapter SIGAW | The Last Echo
!expr normal
MARIA: This time, we go together.
MARK: Together.
!battle sigaw
!end
